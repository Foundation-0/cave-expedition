03//Outside check

Two passes, cold read

What held, what moved.

The outside check worked in two passes. The first pass, an evaluator, stated each claim at its strongest, re-ran the team checker in three Python modes, checked the claim with new code that shares nothing with the team code, read the proof at its load-bearing steps and searched prior work. The second pass, an advocate, took the team side on every finding that narrowed a claim. It rebuilt the evidence, read the cited prior work in full, and ruled stands, stands narrowed or falls. Where a label moved, it moved because the same result exists under another name in a neighbouring field.

What held: the central sums in the covered rows. What moved: the newness labels. AUDIT-017 kept N2 with medium confidence. AUDIT-009 was rated a strong N1 and a candidate N2. AUDIT-015R was not checked beyond its statement. The other internal N2 labels moved to N1 or N0. The ledger did not rewrite its rows. The rulings sit beside the internal labels in a separate file, the outside rulings index, which this page and the README follow.

A separate outside test then checked the eight-way sentence on its own. It found the stated pattern true of nearly any block system, so the pattern alone cannot link the fields. One pair does link by an exact map: AUDIT-017 with AUDIT-019. The home page states the eight-way claim in that narrower form.

Rows past AUDIT-019. Two later rows, AUDIT-020R (16D and 24D lattice realisations plus the root-graph block) and AUDIT-021 (method and handoff), are recorded in the twin case study and in open draft PR #1. They are not in verification/AUDIT_LOG.md on main yet. Twin links to AUDIT-020 land here until that sync merges. The outside check read AUDIT-020R as N1.
