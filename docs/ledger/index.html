03//Outside check

Two passes, cold read

What held, what moved.

The outside check worked in two passes. The first pass re-ran the central sums and read the cited prior work in full. The second pass re-stated each claimed mechanism in the words of neighbouring fields and searched under those words. A match on mechanism set the label, even when the expedition words differed.

What held: the central sums in the covered rows. What moved: the newness labels. AUDIT-017 kept N2 with medium confidence. AUDIT-009 stayed as a strong N2 candidate. The other internal N2 labels moved to N1 or N0. The ledger did not rewrite its rows. It added the ruling beside each label, which is the form this page copies.

A separate outside test then checked the eight-way sentence on its own. It found the stated pattern true of nearly any block system, so the pattern alone cannot link the fields. One pair does link by an exact map: AUDIT-017 with AUDIT-019. The home page states the eight-way claim in that narrower form.

Rows past AUDIT-019. Two later rows, AUDIT-020R (16D and 24D lattice realisations plus the root-graph block) and AUDIT-021 (method and handoff), are recorded in the twin case study and in open draft PR #1. They are not in verification/AUDIT_LOG.md on main yet. Twin links to AUDIT-020 land here until that sync merges. The outside check read AUDIT-020R as N1. Full text: cave-expedition case study in the Aletheia repo.

How to cite a row. Give the audit id, the oracle class and the outside ruling. Example: AUDIT-017, exact, N2 kept with medium confidence. Do not cite an internal N2 label on its own for any other row.

04//Evidence ladder

Colour plus bars

Read the badge, then the bars.

Colour shows the class of standing. Filled bars show strength inside that class, from one to five. A badge never replaces the ledger row. It points back to it.
