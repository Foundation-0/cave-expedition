04//Five chains

Reading order

Read the ledger in five threads.

The log runs in time order. These five threads run in idea order. Each thread starts at a failure or a wall and ends at the patched result. Follow one thread end to end to see the method work once.

  1. Spectral chain. Forgery in the raw verifier (AUDIT-001) to sharp bound (002) to higher moments (014) to box certifier and dual checker (015R, 016R).
  2. Lattice fold chain. All-dimension fold (AUDIT-009) to E8 landmark (017) to elastic collapse on the same curves (019). The last two share the one exact map the outside check kept.
  3. Continuum chain. Peeling tower and sheaf remainder (AUDIT-004) to omitted-variable law (005) to patched bridges (006R, 007R, 008R).
  4. Gravity chain. Slow-manifold closure (AUDIT-011R) to held-out refutation and chirp repair (012) to box certifier (013R) to witness remainder (018).
  5. Fractal chain. Early fragile probes (AUDIT-003) to zero-log scaling tests on live runs (010), with Kigami rescaling as the routed next build.
