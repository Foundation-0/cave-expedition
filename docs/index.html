Cave Expedition

Cave Expedition is the field record of a first-principles expedition run in November 2026. It holds three things in one place: a map of the raw sources, a map of the seams between live repositories, and an independent ledger that checked every claim. The method that grew out of the run has its own site.

Read this site as the evidence side of the pair. The rules side is Aletheia. Claims here link to ledger rows. Ledger rows link to check scripts you can run. Internal novelty labels are shown next to the outside rulings that later cut most of them down.

01//The turn

Same sources, worked twice

First pass closed empty. Second pass filled the ledger.

The team worked the same raw transcripts twice. The first pass graded rough drafts as finished work and closed with nothing new. One instruction changed the second pass: keep the map maker in the loop and repair each draft before judging it.

In the second pass the map maker stayed active and went back into the raw sources after each surprise. Bugs were logged in full. The team then stated the strongest sound claim near each broken draft and tested that claim with separate code. The checker stopped giving plain yes or no answers and started giving counterexamples and exact walls. Those walls told the team what to build next.

This was a before and after comparison, not a controlled trial. The second pass also ran longer. The joint map shows which cave event forced which rule on the twin site.

03//Standing

Drafts await human review

What the outside check kept.

Domain proofs live in sibling repositories as draft pull requests. They await human review. Nothing in the ledger counts as merged or settled on the word of this site alone.

An outside check re-read the ledger cold. It kept one result as new ground with medium confidence: the continuous fold from the cross-polytope tiling to the E8 lattice, AUDIT-017, with determinant 16 and kissing number 240. It rated one more, the all-dimension fold to Dn, AUDIT-009, as a strong local advance and a candidate for new ground: strong N1, candidate N2. It did not check AUDIT-015R beyond its statement. It moved the other internal N2 labels down to local advances or to known results under other names. The site shows internal labels and outside rulings side by side so the cut stays visible.
